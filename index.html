About Me

I’m a Portuguese filmmaker interested in stories that explore people, emotions and the quiet moments that often go unnoticed.

After graduating in Marketing and International Business, I decided to redirect my career towards filmmaking and audiovisual production. Although my academic background is not in film, I have always been drawn to the way cinema can communicate ideas and emotions through image, sound, movement and silence.

I work across directing, cinematography and editing, developing my own short-form films while building experience within professional productions and sets.

My approach is rooted in visual storytelling. I’m particularly interested in intimate narratives, natural environments, restrained performances and images that leave room for interpretation.

I’m currently developing a series of independent short films as a way of exploring my own visual language, learning through making, and building a body of work that reflects the kind of filmmaker I want to become.

Based in Portugal. Available for film, television and audiovisual productions.

More about me:

I grew up surrounded by diverse visual references, and my curiosity has always driven me toward understanding how images evoke deep emotional responses.

I’m a passionate creative who has always loved cinema, visual arts, and the meticulous process of building a frame from scratch. Every project is an opportunity to learn, collaborate, and push the boundaries of visual language.

STILL

ROLE: DIRECTOR · WRITER · CINEMATOGRAPHER · EDITOR
IN PRODUCTION · 2026

After becoming increasingly frustrated with his attempts to write and create, a young filmmaker finds himself trapped in a cycle of doubt and isolation. Unable to move forward, he leaves his familiar surroundings and travels away for a brief escape. But distance does not necessarily provide an answer. Eventually, he has to return to the same place where he started — and face the unfinished story once again.

STILL is an independent short film exploring creative frustration, isolation and the quiet decision to keep going when nothing seems to be working.
