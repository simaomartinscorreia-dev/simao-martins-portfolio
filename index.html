About Me

I’m 21 years old, natural from Paços de Ferreira in the Porto district, Portugal. Holder of a degree in Marketing and International Business, my mission is centered on transforming brand presence and creating high-value audiovisual content that drives business growth while consistently exceeding client expectations.

My background in marketing gives me a strategic mindset: I analyze consumer behavior, market positioning, and brand identity. Meanwhile, my passion for filmmaking, directing, cinematography, and editing allows me to translate those strategic goals into visual narratives that evoke deep emotional responses through thoughtful frames, pacing, and sound design.

During my professional internship, I honed my hands-on experience in real-world workflows, client relations, and visual communication. Parallel to this, I continuously develop independent short films and spec commercials as personal projects to refine my creative vision and push artistic boundaries.

Driven by relentless ambition, my goal is to combine corporate brand transformation with cinema-level visual standards, ultimately aspiring to collaborate with global production powerhouses and top-tier entertainment companies like Netflix, Amazon Prime, Marvel Studios, and Warner Bros.

Based in Portugal. Available for marketing strategy, brand content, and audiovisual productions. Let's elevate your brand together.

STILL

ROLE: DIRECTOR · WRITER · CINEMATOGRAPHER · EDITOR
IN PRODUCTION · 2026

After becoming increasingly frustrated with his attempts to write and create, a young filmmaker finds himself trapped in a cycle of doubt and isolation. Unable to move forward, he leaves his familiar surroundings and travels away for a brief escape. But distance does not necessarily provide an answer. Eventually, he has to return to the same place where he started — and face the unfinished story once again.

STILL is an independent short film exploring creative frustration, isolation and the quiet decision to keep going when nothing seems to be working.
